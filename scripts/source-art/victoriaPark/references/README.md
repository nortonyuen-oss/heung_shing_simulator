## Map relationships to preserve

The map's compass is rotated: the top of the image is not precisely north. Use the major roads and neighbouring facilities as stronger placement references.

- Harbour / Island Eastern Corridor / Victoria Park Road side: wooded gardens and Hill Knoll Plaza; model boat pool and bandstand toward Gloucester Road; bowling greens and playgrounds farther toward Hing Fat Street.
- Centre: central lawn and jogging loop toward Gloucester Road; round Water Fountain Plaza immediately outside its west end. Tennis centre court and surrounding standard courts to the east of the lawn.
- Hing Fat Street side: swimming pool complex on the park's outer edge, next to tennis.
- Causeway Road / Central Library side: six soccer pitches in a long row. Queen Victoria statue is on the central approach between the two groups of three pitches. Basketball courts at the Hing Fat Street end.

## Six-module interpretation

Each 4 × 4 module at 20 m per tile covers 80 m × 80 m. Two columns by three rows cover 160 m × 240 m = 38,400 square metres = 3.84 hectares. The actual park exceeds 19 hectares; this target is therefore a condensed scenic interpretation, rather than a survey-scale replica. The module arrangement below preserves the principal adjacencies of the official map:

| Row from harbour side | Left column | Right column |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Wooded garden, model boat pool, bandstand | Bowling greens, playgrounds, garden paths |
| 2 | Central lawn, jogging loop, circular fountain plaza | Tennis centre/courts; indoor swimming complex at outer edge |
| 3 | West group of soccer pitches | East group of soccer pitches, basketball at outer edge; statue on central southern approach |

Court counts and dimensions require selective simplification at this area. Ground projection height:width of 1:2 is a separate isometric rendering requirement, not a statement that the park is being recreated at half its real dimensions.
